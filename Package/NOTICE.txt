DMC5 DualSense Layer is an unofficial community modification for the Windows
Steam version of Devil May Cry 5. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or
supported by Capcom, Sony Interactive Entertainment, or Valve.

Users must own a legitimate Steam copy of Devil May Cry 5. The installer does
not include the game executable, saves, PAK payloads, or any mechanism for
bypassing ownership, DRM, or platform authentication.

The release bundles or installs open-source third-party components under their
respective licenses in the Licenses directory. Sony, PlayStation, DualSense,
Devil May Cry, and related names and marks belong to their respective owners.

Back up important files and use the mod at your own risk. The installer keeps a
manifest and backups for reversible removal, but game or Steam updates can
change compatibility.
